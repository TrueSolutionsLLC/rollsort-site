Privacy policy

Effective November 24, 2026. This policy covers the Rollsort iOS app and these support pages.

Your Photos library

Rollsort uses Apple’s Photos framework to display the photos and videos you authorize. Dates, album membership and available location information help organize your roll. Similarity and blur checks run on the device. Apple Photos can retrieve iCloud previews according to your settings. The Places view uses Apple Maps.

What the developer receives

Rollsort has no user account, advertising SDKs, analytics SDKs or developer-operated photo server. Your library, review choices and photo identifiers stay in the app’s local storage or in Apple Photos. Rollsort does not transmit them to the developer or sell them.

Choices, storage and deletion

Review decisions, queued identifiers and activity history are kept in local app storage until updated or until you delete the app and its data. Device backups are handled by your operating system. Remove or limit Photos access in iOS Settings at any time. Deleting Rollsort does not reverse changes you already confirmed in Apple Photos. Apple manages iCloud synchronization and Recently Deleted.

Support and this website

If you email support, the developer receives your email address and the information you choose to include, and uses them to answer your request. Contact us to request deletion of your support correspondence, subject to applicable retention requirements. These pages are hosted by GitHub Pages, which may log technical request information under GitHub’s privacy statement.
