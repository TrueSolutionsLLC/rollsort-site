Rollsort support

Get help sorting the photos and videos in your Apple Photos library.

What does a left swipe do?

It queues the item in Rollsort’s review bin. Open the tray in Library, check the items you want to remove, then tap Delete selected. Rollsort asks you to confirm, and Apple Photos handles the final deletion prompt.

Can I change my mind?

Use the undo arrow during a review. In the bin, uncheck anything you want to leave for later, or choose Keep selected instead. Unchecked items stay in Photos. After a confirmed deletion, open Recently Deleted in Apple Photos to see available recovery options.

Where is my progress?

Rollsort saves decisions on your device. Open an unfinished month or tap Resume in Library to continue a recent review. The sample roll has its own history.

Why are some photos missing?

With limited Photos access, Rollsort shows the items you share with it. In Rollsort Settings, choose Manage selected photos to change that selection. Items stored in iCloud may need a connection to load a preview.
